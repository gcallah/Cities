Part 3

I Dreamed a Dream (a planner's lament)

Urban planning modernism: Howard, Le Corbusier and Wright

Goes with: Urban Planning Modernism

I dreamed a dream of cities high
Very beautiful but not for living
I dreamed that my plan would never die
That the city council would keep on giving

Then I was young and unafraid
And cities were made and used and wasted
There was no penalty to be paid
No plaza unbuilt, no slums acerbated

But Jane Jacobs comes at night
With her voice soft as thunder
As she tears my plan apart
As she turns my scheme to shame

Cities, Culture and Economy: lectures, handouts and quizzes from a course on cities, from the first towns to Jane Jacobs.
